full: A title that, on its own, tells the full event and its nature.
partial: A title that is context or time sensitive, naming specific news, a result, an event or an announcement, but that on its own does not tell the full event and its nature.
nothing: A title that isn't about serious tennis news or discussion (fun posts, fan activities, memes), so on its own it tells nothing about a tennis event, whatever names it mentions.
